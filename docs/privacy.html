Privacy policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026 · App: Notiq · Developer: TrexoLab

Notiq is a desktop app that runs on your computer. We do not run a server for it, and we do not receive your notes, files or account details.

What stays on your device

  • Your notes, whiteboards and settings are stored locally.
  • Vault notes are encrypted on your device with a key derived from your master password. The password and the decrypted key are never sent anywhere.
  • Notiq has no analytics, crash reporting or advertising, and the developers run no server that receives your data.

Vault Sync (optional)

Vault Sync is optional and off by default. It syncs your vault only. Your plain notes are never synced. In version 0.9.0 the only storage option is Google Drive.

You can turn it on in Settings → Vault → Sync. If you never connect it, Notiq sends nothing to Google.

Google user data

If you connect Google Drive, Notiq requests these scopes:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata: access to Notiq's own hidden app folder in your Google Drive. Notiq cannot see or touch any other file in your Drive.
  • openid and email: your Google account email address, used only to show "Connected as …".

What is stored in Drive. Only encrypted objects:

  • vault metadata: your vault key wrapped by your master password and recovery key, and the key-derivation parameters;
  • one encrypted snapshot per device;
  • encrypted attachment blobs.

Encryption. Everything is encrypted end to end with your vault key, on your device, before it is uploaded. Google and the Notiq developer cannot read note names, contents or attachments. Only object sizes and timestamps are visible.

Sign-in tokens. The Google refresh token is stored only in your operating system's keychain on your device. It is never sent anywhere except to Google. Your email address is saved only in a local settings file on your device.

How we use and share it. Only to store and fetch your encrypted vault so your devices stay in step. Notiq has no server: it talks directly from your computer to Google, and the developer receives nothing. Google user data is not shared, sold or transferred, and is not used for advertising or to train AI or machine-learning models.

Stop, revoke and delete.

  • In Notiq: Settings → Vault → Sync → Disconnect. This removes the token and email from your device.
  • Revoke Notiq's access at myaccount.google.com/connections.
  • Delete the hidden app data: Google Drive on the web → Settings → Manage apps → Notiq → Delete hidden app data.

If you lose both your master password and recovery key, nobody, including us, can recover your vault.

Notiq's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Optional AI features

AI is off by default. When you turn it on, only the text needed for a request is sent to the Ollama endpoint you configure: your own machine, or Ollama Cloud if you add an API key. Nothing is sent while AI is off, and locked vault notes are never sent.

What we can see

Nothing. The developers have no access to your notes, your vault or your Google Drive.

Children

Notiq is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from anyone.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with an updated date. Questions or concerns: open an issue at github.com/trexolab-solution/notiq/issues.
