Your notes and secrets, kept on your own machine.

Notiq is a fast desktop Markdown editor, whiteboard and knowledge graph. Passwords, SSH keys and private notes go into a Secure Vault: one encrypted file that never leaves your machine.

Download Notiq View source

Free and open source (MIT) for Windows, macOS and Linux. No account, no telemetry.

A Markdown editor with VS Code’s engine underneath

Notiq’s editor is Monaco, tuned for Markdown. Write in Source, read in Preview, or keep both side by side in Split. Tables, task lists, Mermaid diagrams, LaTeX and [[wiki links]] all render as you type.

  • Multi-cursor, find and replace, folding, every shortcut you already know
  • Tabs that remember cursor and scroll position between launches
  • Files stay plain Markdown on your own disk

What the vault guarantees

      
Notiq runs no server, so every protection lives on your machine. This is exactly what it does to keep a locked vault locked.

    

    
    
      
  • One encrypted file
    Names, folders, tags and contents all sit inside vault.nqdb. The key comes from your master password through Argon2id; data is sealed with XChaCha20-Poly1305.
    • 
      
  • Recovery key, shown once
    At setup you get a recovery key. Keep it offline. There is no server and no one who can reset your password for you.
    • 
      
  • Locks itself
    Auto-lock after inactivity, lock when the window closes, and a panic lock on Ctrl+Alt+L.
    • 
      
  • Clipboard cleared
    Anything you copy from the vault is wiped from the clipboard after 20 seconds.
    • 
      
  • Keys wiped from memory
    When the vault locks, the decrypted key material is zeroized rather than left for the garbage collector.
    • 
      
  • Kept apart from everything else
    Vault items never appear in search, the knowledge graph or the AI assistant. AI is never given vault content.
    • 
      
  • Nothing leaves your device
    Notiq has no account system, keeps no cloud copy and sends no telemetry. Your vault exists only where you put it.
    • 
    

    

      
      
The honest limit. Anyone who gets a copy of vault.nqdb can try to guess your master password offline. Argon2id makes every guess slow and expensive, but it cannot save a short or reused password. Use a long, unique passphrase.
