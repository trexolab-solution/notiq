A vault for the notes you wouldn't paste into a chat.

Lock any note and it becomes an encrypted .nqv file in a folder you choose. Group them: SSH, Logins, Recovery codes, whatever you use.

Strong by default. Argon2id turns your master password into a key; XChaCha20-Poly1305 encrypts every note. A separate recovery key is shown once, at setup.

Auto-lock after idle time or when the app goes to the tray, plus a panic shortcut you can set yourself.

Sync it across machines without trusting a server.

Connect your own Telegram bot and the vault syncs both ways between your computers. Only ciphertext is uploaded, under random file names, so titles and folders never leave the app. Google Drive is next.

On a new machine: install Notiq, choose Restore from Telegram, enter the bot token and your master password. That's it.

The bot token lives in your system keychain. Conflicts keep both versions; nothing is ever deleted by sync.

Everything else you'd expect

Editor
Monaco-based Markdown editing with live preview, tabs and a built-in terminal.
Whiteboard
Sketch next to your notes; drawings stay with the tab.
Knowledge graph
Link notes with wiki-style links and see how they connect.
Local-first
Files on your disk. AI is optional, off by default, and only talks to the Ollama endpoint you configure.

Get started

  1. Download the installer for your system from the latest release.
  2. Write as usual. When a note is private, lock it and pick a vault folder.
  3. Optional: connect Telegram in Settings → Vault to keep your other machines in step.
